# Humi / Employment Hero Canada

> humi.ca is the Canadian HR, payroll, benefits and hiring platform that is now part of Employment Hero (humi.ca redirects to employmenthero.com/en-ca). The platform provides an all-in-one Employment Operating System — HR management, payroll, applicant tracking, employee experience, and Employer-of-Record hiring — and exposes a public REST API secured with OAuth 2.0 (authorization-code + PKCE).
